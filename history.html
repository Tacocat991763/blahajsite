History of the Blahaj

The Blahaj is a blue shark plush made by IKEA. It has become a popular internet meme and LGBTQ+ symbol.

The blahaj first originated around 2007 as a dark blue shark plush, it stayed this way until 2008 when it was redesigned to a grey shark. The last photo of a grey blahaj is from 2012.

Blahaj as it is known today first appeared in 2011. However, somewhere before 2012 it got discontinued and was rereleased in 2014, it has stayed on IKEA's shelves since then.

In 2014 memes showing Blahaj doing everyday tasks became popular on Tumblr, then in 2018 similar memes began to blow up on Russian social media platforms.

In 2020, the Blahaj gained even more popularity, the pandemic caused many people to see the memes and want a plush to hug (if you can't hug people, hug a haj).

Also in 2020, the Blahaj began to gain traction on Tumblr again, except this time with the trans community, quickly become an unofficial trans icon.

Since 2020, the Blahaj has cemented its place in trans culture. It has become a comfort, a way to descretly express your identity, and symbol of the trans community.
